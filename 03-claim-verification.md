## Things I deliberately left out because I could not verify them

- Any share price number for either company in any year after 1717.
- Any EIC dividend rate for any year. The usual figure I had in mind from memory is not in a source I read.
- A ransom figure for Madras in 1746. Forrest and Grose are cited in a footnote on the Royal Historical Society stub page as giving 1,100,000 pagodas (£421,666), but I only saw that footnote, not the pages.
- Anything about the Battle of Plassey or the Treaty of Allahabad. Those belong to 1757 and 1765. The opencode draft placed them in 1727 and 1748, which is wrong, and the letter should not contain them.
- Anything about the VOC being bankrupt in 1702 or ever, before 1750. The VOC survived until 1799. Koot's account is of declining returns, not bankruptcy.
