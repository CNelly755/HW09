## Draft 1: the opencode draft (the "before" version)

Cameron generated a first draft with the opencode tool. I read it against the sources and found the following problems. It is preserved here as the record of the review step, and none of its text survives in the final paper.

Summary of the review: of the factual claims I could check, most were wrong. The bibliography appears fabricated. The draft's recommendation (buy English, sell April 1746) is similar to mine in direction but its supporting figures were invented, and its sale date falls just before Madras fell in September 1746, which it did not mention.

## Draft 2: the rebuilt paper
